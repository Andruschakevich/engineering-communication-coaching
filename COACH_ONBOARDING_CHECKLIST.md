# Coach Onboarding Checklist

A one-page readiness check for coaches preparing to start a new engineering communication coaching engagement.

> **How to use:** Complete this checklist before inviting the participant to the kick-off. You do not need to read the entire repository first. Use the links only when you need more detail. Adapt the framework to the participant and organization; do not treat it as a rigid script.

## 1. Understand the engagement

- [ ] I can explain the purpose of the coaching engagement and the outcomes it is intended to support.
- [ ] I understand the participant's role, seniority, working context, and the development need that prompted the engagement.
- [ ] I have reviewed the [Coaching Principles](COACHING_PRINCIPLES.md), [Program Overview](PROGRAM_OVERVIEW.md), and [Coaching Framework](docs/coaching-framework.md).
- [ ] I understand the boundaries: this is developmental coaching, not therapy, investigation, disciplinary action, or a substitute for performance management.

## 2. Align with the sponsor and organization

- [ ] The coach, participant, and manager/sponsor roles are clear.
- [ ] The participant's involvement is voluntary, and the participant will own their goals and development actions.
- [ ] The sponsor and I have agreed on the purpose, expected time commitment, and what high-level progress information may be shared.
- [ ] We have explicitly agreed that private reflections, raw messages, and session notes are not shared by default.
- [ ] I have checked applicable company policies for meeting recording, transcription, AI tools, and workplace communication data.

**Reference:** [Manager Alignment Guide](pilot-toolkit/manager-alignment.md) · [Confidentiality & Ethics](docs/confidentiality-and-ethics.md)

## 3. Prepare privacy, recording, and AI boundaries

- [ ] I know which meeting/recording/transcription tools are approved and where any recordings or notes may be stored.
- [ ] I will explain the purpose, provider, processing, storage, access, retention, and deletion arrangements before recording.
- [ ] I will obtain explicit consent from every participant before each recording; if anyone declines, the meeting will continue without recording.
- [ ] I understand that consent to record does not automatically authorize third-party transcription or AI processing.
- [ ] I will not place raw work correspondence, chat exports, recordings, transcripts, customer data, or identifiable coaching records in this reusable repository.
- [ ] Any AI-assisted communication review will use only company-approved tools and permitted data. Raw messages will not be shared with the coach or sponsor unless a separate, explicit, policy-compliant agreement permits it.

**Reference:** [Confidentiality & Ethics](docs/confidentiality-and-ethics.md) · [AI-assisted Communication Review Prompt](pilot-toolkit/ai-communication-review-prompt.md)

## 4. Prepare the kick-off

- [ ] I have reviewed the [Kick-off Presentation & Checklist](pilot-toolkit/kickoff-presentation.md).
- [ ] I have the [Initial Meeting Script](pilot-toolkit/initial-meeting-script.md) and [Coaching Agreement](pilot-toolkit/coaching-agreement.md) ready.
- [ ] I have planned to ask about the participant's expectations, goals, concerns, preferences, and feedback—not just present the program.
- [ ] I have allowed time to agree on session dates, communication channels, rescheduling, and the next steps.
- [ ] I have a suitable private, approved place to keep any engagement-specific notes and assessment materials.

## 5. Prepare the baseline and delivery materials

- [ ] I have reviewed the [Competency Model](docs/competency-model.md) and [Baseline Assessment](pilot-toolkit/baseline-assessment.md).
- [ ] I know how self-assessment, manager observations, coach observations, and optional AI-assisted assessment will be used and explained.
- [ ] I will assess observable behaviors, not personality or presumed intent; use N/O when evidence is insufficient.
- [ ] I will not average perspectives into one score or treat an AI output as an objective verdict.
- [ ] I have opened the [Curriculum](curriculum/README.md) and [Facilitator Guide](facilitator-guide/README.md), and know where the eight session plans are.
- [ ] I have the [Session Agenda Template](templates/session-agenda.md), [Weekly Progress Tracker](pilot-toolkit/weekly-progress-tracker.md), and [90-Day Development Plan](templates/90-day-development-plan.md) available.

## 6. Confirm readiness to begin

- [ ] The participant understands the purpose, voluntary nature, expectations, and confidentiality boundaries.
- [ ] The kick-off is scheduled and the participant knows what to expect.
- [ ] The first session dates or an agreed scheduling approach are in place.
- [ ] There is a clear plan for the baseline and for agreeing 2–3 participant-owned development priorities.
- [ ] I know the first small action the participant can take after the kick-off.

**Ready to start when:** the purpose and roles are clear, privacy boundaries are understood, the kick-off is prepared, and the participant can make an informed choice to take part. Not every optional assessment or AI-assisted activity needs to be completed before the first meeting.

---

## After the kick-off

Move from preparation into delivery using the [End-to-End Coaching Journey in the README](README.md#the-end-to-end-coaching-journey). Keep participant-specific records in approved private storage and contribute only anonymized, reusable lessons to this repository.
