# Program Overview

## Mission
Develop observable communication behaviors that help engineering professionals collaborate effectively, handle disagreement, explain ideas, and build trust.

## Audience
Senior individual contributors, SDETs, tech leads, and engineers preparing for broader influence. The program can be used proactively or to address specific development needs.

## Outcomes
Participants learn to recognize communication impact, communicate respectfully, listen actively, give and receive feedback, structure technical explanations, present ideas, resolve disagreement, facilitate decisions, and sustain a personal development plan.

## Format
- Eight sessions, normally weekly; 60–75 minutes each.
- One practical assignment between sessions.
- Baseline, midpoint, endline, and optional 30–90-day follow-up.
- Adapt pace and sequence to the participant; eight weeks is not a guarantee of complete transformation.

## Standard 75-minute session
| Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Check-in | 10 min | Recent situations and reflection |
| Homework review | 10 min | What was tried and what happened |
| Concept / model | 15 min | One focused principle |
| Practice | 25 min | Roleplay, rewrite, presentation, or simulation |
| Feedback / reflection | 10 min | Strength and improvement |
| Commitment | 5 min | One action before next session |

## Working agreement
Align goals, roles, time commitment, homework expectations, confidentiality boundaries, manager updates, and review dates before starting.

## Success
Success means observable, sustained improvement against agreed goals—not merely completing sessions or achieving a high self-rating. Durable behavior change requires continued practice.
