# Active Listening Exercises

1. **Paraphrase first:** one person explains for two minutes; listener summarizes before offering a view. Speaker confirms or corrects.
2. **Clarifying questions only:** for three minutes, ask open questions; no advice or counterarguments.
3. **Listen for interests:** identify whether a proposed solution reflects reliability, speed, cost, ownership, learning, or risk reduction.
4. **Receive feedback:** listen without interruption, ask for an example, paraphrase, and identify one action.

Reflect: What was difficult about not responding immediately? What did you learn? How did the other person react?
