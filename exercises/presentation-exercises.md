# Presentation Exercises

## One topic, three audiences
Explain one technical issue to a senior engineer (3 min), product manager (2 min), and executive (1 min).

## Five-minute proposal
Structure: problem, why it matters, proposed solution, evidence/trade-offs, expected outcome, next step.

## Impromptu response
Speak for 60–90 seconds using PREP on one prompt:
- Test reliability or execution speed?
- Refactor or add a feature?
- What makes a technical decision reversible?
- How should teams handle recurring incidents?

## Q&A pressure test
The coach asks a clarifying, skeptical, and out-of-scope question. Practice answering directly, acknowledging uncertainty, and offering a follow-up.
