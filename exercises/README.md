# Practice Exercises

Use these exercises to turn concepts into observable communication behaviors. Select exercises based on the participant's goals and current session; do not assign the entire collection.

> **Coach navigation:** Follow the [main README](../README.md) for the complete coaching journey. Use the [Curriculum](../curriculum/README.md) to choose the relevant session, then select one or two exercises below.

## How to select an exercise

1. Start with one agreed development goal.
2. Choose an exercise that practices the target behavior directly.
3. Explain the scenario and success criteria.
4. Let the participant attempt it before offering a model answer.
5. Give specific feedback on behavior and impact.
6. Repeat the exercise with one adjustment.
7. Agree where the participant can practice the behavior at work.

## Facilitation reminders

- Use fictional or sanitized examples; do not expose private work conversations.
- Keep practice psychologically safe while giving candid, actionable feedback.
- Do not equate one roleplay performance with a stable trait or ability.
- Adapt difficulty to the participant's experience and readiness.
