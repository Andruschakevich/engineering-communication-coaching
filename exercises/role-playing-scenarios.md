# Role-Playing Scenarios

## Facilitation protocol
Explain the learning objective; assign roles; prepare briefly; roleplay for 3–5 minutes; ask the participant what they noticed; give specific feedback; repeat; increase difficulty gradually.

## Scenarios
1. **Technical disagreement:** defend a proposal without dismissing the colleague. Look for fair paraphrase, evidence, questions, options, and a shared goal.
2. **Colleague made a mistake:** explain impact and restore service without humiliation. Focus on facts, recovery, and prevention.
3. **Offensive joke:** a colleague says a joke was disrespectful. Acknowledge impact, apologize without qualification, and commit to change.
4. **Critical feedback:** a manager says several teammates find communication dismissive. Ask for examples, paraphrase, and agree on actions.
5. **Executive presentation:** explain business value, cost, alternatives, and risk in plain language.
6. **Tense meeting:** two engineers interrupt each other while a third stays silent. Facilitate, include, summarize, and close with a decision.
7. **Priority conflict:** Product wants speed; Engineering sees quality or operational risk. Make trade-offs explicit and offer options.

## Difficulty progression
Level 1 cooperative; Level 2 disagreement; Level 3 interruptions or challenged assumptions; Level 4 time pressure and ambiguity; Level 5 multiple stakeholders with competing goals.

Do not provoke for its own sake. Practice self-regulation and clarity, not embarrassment.
