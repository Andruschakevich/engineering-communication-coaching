# Reflection Exercises

## After-action review
What was the situation? What outcome did I want? What did I say or do? What happened? What worked? What would I change?

## Trigger mapping
Which situations trigger defensiveness? What early signs do I notice? What pause or question could help? What response do I want to choose?

## Impact check
How might the other person have interpreted my words? What evidence supports that? What alternative explanations exist? Is a repair conversation appropriate?

## Weekly review
Choose one behavior to maintain, one to improve, and one experiment for next week.
