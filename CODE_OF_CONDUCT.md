# Program Code of Conduct

All participants, coaches, managers, and invited stakeholders agree to:
- Treat others with dignity and professional respect.
- Challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Avoid discriminatory, humiliating, threatening, or harassing behavior.
- Listen without routinely interrupting or dismissing others.
- Give feedback about observable behavior and impact.
- Respect confidentiality and data-minimization rules.
- Consider cultural context, accessibility, and different communication norms.
- Participate in good faith and follow through on commitments.
- Acknowledge mistakes and make reasonable efforts to repair harm.

If the agreement is breached, pause, describe the behavior and impact, and agree on a corrective step. Serious or repeated workplace concerns must be handled through established management, HR, or safety processes.
