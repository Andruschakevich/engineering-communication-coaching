# Additional Learning Materials

Explore short, focused materials on:
- Active and reflective listening.
- SBI feedback (Situation–Behavior–Impact).
- BLUF and executive communication.
- PREP for concise explanations.
- Conflict resolution and interest-based negotiation.
- Meeting facilitation and inclusive participation.
- Emotional self-regulation and repair after communication mistakes.

Prefer materials with practical examples and exercises. Avoid presenting personality tests or communication-style labels as diagnoses or fixed truths.
