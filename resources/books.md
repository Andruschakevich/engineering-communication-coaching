# Books

Suggested order:
1. **Crucial Conversations** — high-stakes conversations and dialogue under pressure.
2. **Nonviolent Communication** by Marshall B. Rosenberg — observations, feelings, needs, and requests.
3. **How to Win Friends and Influence People** by Dale Carnegie — interpersonal relationships; adapt critically to modern contexts.
4. **Talk Like TED** by Carmine Gallo — presentation structure and delivery.

Choose one idea and practice it. No single book is a universal method.
