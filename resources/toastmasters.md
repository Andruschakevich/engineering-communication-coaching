# Toastmasters

Toastmasters International offers club-based practice in public speaking, impromptu speaking, and feedback.

1. Find nearby clubs with the [official club finder](https://www.toastmasters.org/Find-a-Club).
2. Visit two meetings as a guest.
3. If the environment suits you, consider joining.
4. Deliver an Ice Breaker speech.
5. Practice Table Topics: impromptu 1–2 minute responses.
6. Ask for specific feedback and track one improvement at a time.
7. Review progress after about three months.

Toastmasters complements, but does not replace, workplace practice.
