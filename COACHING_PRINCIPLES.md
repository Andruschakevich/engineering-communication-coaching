# Coaching Principles

1. **Partnership:** frame the work as joint development, not a lecture or punishment.
2. **Behavior over identity:** describe observable actions, not personality labels.
3. **Intent and impact:** explore what was intended, what happened, and how it affected others.
4. **Candid kindness:** give specific, proportionate feedback while preserving dignity.
5. **Practice over theory:** rehearse realistic situations and repeat with a better approach.
6. **Actionable feedback:** describe situation, behavior, impact, and a practical alternative.
7. **Participant ownership:** the coach facilitates; the participant chooses and applies changes.
8. **Reinforce progress:** recognize concrete improvements and treat setbacks as learning data.
9. **Role clarity:** coaching is not therapy, investigation, HR adjudication, or performance management.
10. **Responsible measurement:** combine perspectives and examples; avoid false precision.
