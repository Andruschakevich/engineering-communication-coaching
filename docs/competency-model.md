# Competency Model

Rate observable behavior, not personality or presumed intent.

## Rating anchors
| Score | Description |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Rarely demonstrated; behavior is often counterproductive |
| 2 | Inconsistent; understands expectations but needs support |
| 3 | Usually effective in routine situations; struggles under pressure |
| 4 | Consistent, including in difficult situations |
| 5 | Role model; helps others develop the behavior |

A score is a discussion aid, not a scientific measurement. Record examples, context, source, and confidence.

## Competencies and indicators
### Respectful communication
Criticizes ideas rather than people; avoids humiliating humor, insults, contempt, and unnecessary profanity; adjusts tone when discomfort is signaled.

### Emotional awareness and regulation
Recognizes triggers; pauses before reacting; distinguishes disagreement from disrespect; repairs communication when impact is harmful.

### Active listening
Lets others finish; asks clarifying questions; paraphrases accurately; acknowledges valid points even when disagreeing.

### Feedback skills
Receives feedback without immediate dismissal; asks for examples; gives behavior-based feedback; agrees on next steps.

### Structured communication
States the point early; adapts detail to audience; separates facts, assumptions, options, and recommendations; closes with a decision or request.

### Conflict resolution and influencing
Identifies shared goals; presents evidence and trade-offs; explores alternatives; helps reach an explicit decision.

### Presentation and facilitation
Uses a clear structure; explains technical concepts for the audience; handles questions without defensiveness; invites contributions and summarizes outcomes.

### Collaboration and professional presence
Shares context; gives others room to contribute; follows through; disagrees without dominating.

For each rating capture score, examples, context/frequency, source, confidence (low/medium/high), and one behavior to maintain or change. Do not average thin evidence mechanically.
