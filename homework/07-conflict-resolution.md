# Homework 07 — Conflict Resolution in Practice

**Stage:** After Session 6  
**Purpose:** Move a disagreement toward shared understanding, evidence, and a constructive next step.

## Must do

Apply the disagreement sequence in one real, appropriate technical or delivery discussion:

1. State or confirm the shared goal.
2. Summarize the other person's position fairly.
3. Ask about constraints and evidence.
4. State your concern and supporting evidence.
5. Compare options and trade-offs.
6. Agree on a decision, experiment, owner, or escalation path.

Afterward, reflect using a sanitized description. Do not disclose confidential details.

## Nice to do

Rehearse a second, more challenging scenario with the coach or a trusted peer. Increase difficulty with time pressure, interruption, or new evidence.

## Reflection

- Did I understand the other position accurately?
- Did I separate evidence from assumptions?
- Did the conversation become personal? If so, what changed?
- Was there a clear next step and owner?

## Completion standard

The participant can describe how they helped a disagreement progress toward a decision or constructive next step.
