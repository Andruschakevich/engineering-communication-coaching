# Homework 05 — Structured Communication

**Stage:** After Session 4  
**Purpose:** Communicate a complex issue clearly and make the decision or request explicit.

## Must do

Choose one current, non-sensitive technical or delivery issue and prepare it using:

1. **Problem:** What is happening?
2. **Impact:** Why does it matter?
3. **Options:** What realistic choices exist, including trade-offs?
4. **Recommendation:** What do you recommend, and what decision or action is needed?

Use only the detail required by the audience. Ask one colleague from another role whether the message was clear and what remained confusing.

## Nice to do

Explain the same issue to two audiences (for example, an engineer and a product stakeholder) and adapt the level of detail.

## Reflection

- Was the main point clear early?
- Did I separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations?
- Was the request or decision explicit?
- What detail could I remove?

## Completion standard

The participant can deliver a concise, audience-appropriate explanation with a clear recommendation or request.
