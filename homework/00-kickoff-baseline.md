# Homework 00 — Baseline, Self-Reflection, and Goals

**Stage:** After kick-off, before Session 1  
**Purpose:** Establish a starting point, compare perspectives carefully, and identify participant-owned development goals.  
**Estimated time:** 60–120 minutes, spread across the week; agree the scope and due date with the participant.

## Must do

### 1. Complete the self-assessment

Open the shared communication assessment spreadsheet and complete the **Participant rating (1–5)** column. Add short evidence or examples where useful.

### 2. Complete an AI-assisted communication review (only if approved)

Use the [AI-assisted Communication Review Prompt](../pilot-toolkit/ai-communication-review-prompt.md) with an organization-approved tool and only explicitly authorized, appropriate work sources.

- Review the agreed period and sources only.
- An agreed window of up to six months may be useful when permitted and proportionate; narrow the window if a smaller sample is enough. Never collect broad exports by default.
- Do not indiscriminately export or upload all correspondence.
- Exclude personal conversations, credentials, customer secrets, sensitive personal information, HR/medical/legal matters, and restricted channels.
- Confirm company policy, tool/provider approval, account privacy and retention settings before processing.
- Do not share raw correspondence or verbatim examples with the coach. Share only a sanitized summary in the agreed private workspace.
- Treat AI ratings as tentative observations, not objective or formal assessments.

Compare self-assessment and AI-assisted observations. Identify 3–5 meaningful differences, consider alternative explanations, and decide what is worth exploring—not merely which score is higher.

### 3. Reflect independently and draft goals

Answer:
- Which communication situations are easiest for me?
- Which are hardest?
- What feedback have I heard more than once?
- What happens when I am rushed, challenged, or misunderstood?
- What behavior would I like to change or strengthen?
- What might the AI have misunderstood or missed?

Draft 3–5 possible goals using:
- What do I want to improve?
- Why does it matter?
- What behavior will I practice?
- In which situations?
- How will I notice progress?
- What support do I need?

The participant owns the final choice of goals. The coach helps refine them into observable, realistic behaviors.

## Nice to do

### Explore a speaking club

When useful and agreed with the participant, identify a suitable local or online Toastmasters club, check its schedule, contact the organizers, and attend one meeting as a guest. Joining the club is not required. Keep this as an optional extension unless the coach and participant explicitly agree to include it in the current homework.

## Deliverable for the coach

Before Session 1, share only:
- confirmation that the self-assessment is complete;
- a sanitized summary of AI-assisted observations, if approved;
- 3–5 draft goals and the participant's own reflections;
- any questions or concerns to discuss.

Do not send raw message exports, screenshots, or identifiable correspondence.

## Completion standard

The participant can describe their current strengths, identify a few development priorities, and explain why those priorities matter. A numerical score or AI report alone does not complete this assignment.
