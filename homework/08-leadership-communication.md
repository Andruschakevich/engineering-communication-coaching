# Homework 08 — Leadership Communication and Facilitation

**Stage:** After Session 7  
**Purpose:** Facilitate collaboration, make space for different views, and close discussions with clear ownership.

## Must do

Lead a real 15-minute technical or cross-functional discussion. Before the meeting, define its purpose and the decision or outcome needed. During the meeting:
- invite input from more than the most vocal participants;
- summarize agreement and disagreement fairly;
- clarify decisions, owners, and deadlines;
- close by confirming next steps.

Afterward, ask two participants for brief feedback on clarity, inclusion, and facilitation. Keep feedback voluntary and specific.

## Nice to do

Try a second facilitation technique (for example, a round-robin input or silent idea generation) and compare its effect.

## Reflection

- Who contributed, and who may not have had enough space?
- Did I facilitate or dominate?
- Were decisions and owners clear?
- What would I change next time?

## Completion standard

The participant can demonstrate inclusive facilitation and close a discussion with explicit decisions and ownership.
