# Homework Library

A reusable library of participant assignments for the Engineering Communication Coaching Program.

This folder is the single source of truth for homework. Keep reusable instructions here; keep completed participant work, personal reflections, scores, examples, and AI outputs in an approved private workspace—not in this repository.

## How to use this library

- **Must do** — the core assignment required to achieve the intended learning outcome. Agree on a realistic scope and due date with the participant.
- **Nice to do** — an optional extension for participants who have time, interest, and a suitable opportunity. It must never become a hidden requirement.
- The coach may adapt or replace an assignment based on the participant's goals, role, workload, accessibility needs, and privacy constraints.
- Review homework at the next session. Focus on learning and observable behavior, not completion for its own sake.
- Do not ask participants to upload raw workplace correspondence, private messages, recordings, customer information, or confidential company data to this repository.

## Standard assignment format

Every assignment should specify:
1. Session or stage and learning objective.
2. Must do actions.
3. Nice to do extensions.
4. Expected evidence or deliverable.
5. Reflection questions.
6. Estimated time and due date agreed with the participant.
7. Privacy, consent, and tool-use boundaries where relevant.

## Coach's review checklist

- Did the participant understand why the assignment matters?
- Was the scope realistic and agreed?
- Did the participant try the behavior in a real or realistic situation?
- What did they notice about intent, behavior, and impact?
- What should they repeat, adapt, or stop?
- Does the next assignment build on the learning rather than add unnecessary workload?

## Reuse and privacy

The library contains generic instructions only. Never add participant names, completed homework, individual scores, raw messages, identifiable stories, or private coaching notes. Use sanitized, generalizable learning when improving these materials.
