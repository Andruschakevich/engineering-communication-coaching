# Homework 09 — 90-Day Development Plan

**Stage:** After Session 8  
**Purpose:** Sustain behavior change after the formal coaching program.

## Must do

Complete a 90-day plan containing:
- 2–3 participant-owned communication goals;
- the specific behaviors to maintain or develop;
- real situations in which to practice;
- a simple progress signal for each goal;
- likely obstacles and support needed;
- a 30-day self-review date;
- a 60–90-day follow-up with a coach or appropriate manager, if agreed.

Use evidence from the baseline, session reflections, and observable examples. Do not rely on a single score.

## Nice to do

Create a one-page personal communication playbook with:
- strengths to keep using;
- situations that tend to be difficult;
- preferred strategies and phrases;
- early warning signs;
- repair strategies;
- people or resources that can support continued practice.

## Reflection

- Which change am I most proud of?
- What still requires deliberate practice?
- How will I notice if I am slipping into an old pattern?
- What support or feedback will help me sustain progress?

## Completion standard

The participant leaves with a realistic plan, clear practice opportunities, and scheduled opportunities to review progress.
