# Homework 02 — Communication Diary

**Stage:** After Session 1  
**Purpose:** Notice communication patterns in real situations and connect context, behavior, and impact.  
**Estimated time:** 10–15 minutes per entry across the week.

## Must do

Record 3–5 work situations during the week, including at least one positive interaction.

For each situation, note:
1. Context and what you wanted to achieve.
2. What the other person said or did (briefly, without copying private messages).
3. What you said or did.
4. What happened next or what impact you could observe.
5. What you would keep or change.

Include both successful and difficult interactions. Use sanitized descriptions and do not copy entire private chat threads.

## Nice to do

For each situation, draft one alternative response that preserves your point while improving clarity, respect, or collaboration.

## Reflection

- Which situations triggered the strongest reaction?
- What patterns repeat?
- What worked well?
- What would I like to test next time?

## Completion standard

The participant can identify at least one recurring pattern and one behavior to continue or experiment with.
