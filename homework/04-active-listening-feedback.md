# Homework 04 — Active Listening and Constructive Feedback

**Stage:** After Session 3  
**Purpose:** Improve understanding before responding and practice behavior-based feedback.

## Must do

In three conversations:
1. Let the other person finish.
2. Ask at least two clarifying questions.
3. Paraphrase the key point before offering a solution or disagreement.
4. Check that your understanding is accurate.

Also prepare two appropriate examples of feedback using **Situation – Behavior – Impact** and a constructive next step. Use real, low-risk situations and describe observable behavior rather than personality.

## Nice to do

Ask one trusted colleague whether they felt heard in a recent conversation. Invite a specific example rather than a general rating.

## Reflection

- What changed when I clarified before responding?
- Which question was most useful?
- How did I react when receiving feedback?
- What would make my feedback easier to act on?

## Completion standard

The participant can demonstrate active listening and deliver feedback that is specific, respectful, and actionable.
