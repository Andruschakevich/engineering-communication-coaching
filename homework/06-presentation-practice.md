# Homework 06 — Presentation Practice

**Stage:** After Session 5  
**Purpose:** Build confidence and clarity in a short professional presentation.

## Must do

1. Prepare and record a five-minute presentation on a relevant, non-confidential work topic.
2. Structure it around the audience, problem/opportunity, why it matters, proposed approach, evidence and trade-offs, expected outcome, and next step.
3. Watch the recording and assess clarity, structure, pace, and handling of questions.
4. Identify one strength and one improvement for the next attempt.

Do not record other people or disclose confidential company information without authorization.

## Nice to do

- Ask a trusted colleague for one specific piece of feedback.
- Attend a Toastmasters meeting as a guest and observe how speakers structure their message and handle questions.

## Reflection

- Was the purpose clear in the opening?
- Did the detail fit the audience?
- Where did I lose clarity or confidence?
- What will I change in the next presentation?

## Completion standard

The participant can identify a concrete improvement from reviewing their own presentation and demonstrate a clearer second attempt.
