# Homework 03 — Respectful Communication Practice

**Stage:** After Session 2  
**Purpose:** Express disagreement and concerns directly without sarcasm, labels, or unnecessary personal intensity.

## Must do

1. For one working week, avoid sarcasm and jokes at another person's expense in work settings.
2. Rewrite 10 draft messages or phrases that could sound dismissive, blaming, or unnecessarily sharp.
3. Preserve the substantive concern while making the request, evidence, or next step explicit.
4. Bring three rewritten examples (sanitized) to the next session.

## Nice to do

Ask a trusted colleague for one specific observation about clarity and respectfulness. Do not ask them to rate your personality or disclose other people's private feedback.

## Reflection

- Which wording habits were hardest to change?
- Did directness suffer, or did the message become clearer?
- What did I notice about intent versus impact?
- What will I keep practicing?

## Completion standard

The participant can demonstrate a direct, respectful alternative to a dismissive or sarcastic phrase.
