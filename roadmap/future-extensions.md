# Future Extensions

Potential extensions, subject to pilot feedback:
- Written communication and asynchronous collaboration.
- Senior IC track: technical influence, architecture discussions, mentoring, cross-team alignment.
- Tech lead track: facilitation, delegation, feedback, conflict resolution.
- Engineering manager track: one-to-ones, performance conversations, team norms, organizational influence.
- Remote collaboration: written clarity, timezone-aware communication, inclusive meetings.
- Train-the-coach: facilitator competencies, observation, and quality standards.

Do not add complexity until the core program is useful and consistently deliverable.
