# Behavior-Based Feedback

## SBI
- **Situation:** When and where did it happen?
- **Behavior:** What did the person say or do that an observer could describe?
- **Impact:** What effect did it have on you, the team, or the work?

## Request
What would you like to see next time? What support or clarification would help?

## Response
What do you agree with? What context should be considered? What will you try?
