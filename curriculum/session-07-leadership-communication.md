# Session 7 — Leadership Communication and Professional Presence

**Objectives:** Facilitate rather than dominate, include quieter participants, delegate ownership, and build trust through follow-through.

## Key behaviors
Set the meeting purpose; invite different views; summarize agreement and disagreement; clarify decisions, owners, and deadlines; share context and credit; avoid using expertise to shut down discussion.

## Exercise
Facilitate a 15-minute technical meeting. The coach introduces two opposing views and one quiet participant. Keep the discussion productive and conclude with decisions and owners.

## Homework
Lead a real 15-minute technical discussion. Ask two participants for brief feedback on clarity, inclusion, and facilitation.
