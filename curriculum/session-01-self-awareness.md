# Session 1 — Self-Awareness and Assessment

**Duration:** 60–75 minutes

## Objectives
Establish trust; understand the participant's perspective; explore intent vs. impact; select 2–3 priorities and establish a baseline.

## Preparation
Complete the initial assessment. Bring 2–3 recent situations: one successful, one difficult, and one ambiguous. Only use examples that may appropriately be discussed.

## Agenda
- Working agreement and expectations — 10 min.
- Self-assessment — 15 min.
- Situation analysis — 20 min.
- Baseline and priorities — 15 min.
- First commitment — 5 min.

## Prompts
What does excellent collaboration look like? When does your communication work well? What feedback recurs? What happens when you feel challenged? Which behavior would make the biggest difference?

## Exercise: intent vs. impact
For one situation, record context, intent, observable words/actions, response or impact, evidence, and an alternative behavior.
