# Session 2 — Respectful Communication and Emotional Intelligence

**Objectives:** Understand how humor, sarcasm, profanity, and tone affect trust; disagree without belittling; identify triggers; practice repair.

## Key concepts
- Intent matters, but does not erase impact.
- Humor should not rely on humiliation, stereotypes, power imbalance, or another person's discomfort.
- Assertiveness is direct, honest, and respectful—not passive or aggressive.
- Repair means acknowledging impact, apologizing without qualification, and stating what will change.

## Practice
Rewrite 10 blunt or dismissive statements while preserving the substantive concern.

Example: Instead of "Did you even test this?" say "I found a failure in this path. What tests were run, and can we review the coverage?"

## Roleplay
A colleague says: "That joke was disrespectful." Respond without "it was just a joke" or "you're too sensitive". Practice: "I hear you. I didn't intend to offend you, but I can see the impact. I'm sorry. I'll be more careful."

## Homework
For one week, avoid sarcasm and jokes at another person's expense in work settings. Rewrite 10 draft messages or phrases. Note one trigger and how you responded.
