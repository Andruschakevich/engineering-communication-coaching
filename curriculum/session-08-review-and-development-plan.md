# Session 8 — Review and 90-Day Development Plan

**Objectives:** Review progress against baseline goals, identify remaining challenges, gather proportionate endline feedback, and create a sustainable plan.

## Agenda
- Participant reflection — 10 min.
- Homework and evidence review — 10 min.
- Repeat competency scorecard — 15 min.
- Compare baseline and endline examples — 15 min.
- Stakeholder feedback, if agreed — 10 min.
- 90-day plan — 10–15 min.

## Review questions
What change are you proud of? Which situations remain difficult? What feedback surprised you? What evidence suggests colleagues experience a difference? Which habit will you continue?

## Homework
Complete the 90-day development plan. Schedule 30-day self-review and 60–90-day check-in with an appropriate manager or coach.
