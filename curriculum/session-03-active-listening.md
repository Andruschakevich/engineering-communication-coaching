# Session 3 — Active Listening and Constructive Feedback

**Objectives:** Listen to understand; clarify and paraphrase; give behavior-based feedback; receive feedback without immediate defensiveness.

## Listening sequence
1. Stop preparing a rebuttal.
2. Let the speaker finish.
3. Ask an open clarifying question.
4. Paraphrase the key point.
5. Check that you understood.
6. Respond to the substance.

Useful prompts: "Can you give me a specific example?" "What was the impact?" "What would you have preferred?" "Let me check that I understood..."

## Feedback model: SBI
Situation — when and where. Behavior — what the person said or did, without judgment. Impact — effect on the person, team, or work. Add a practical request or next step.

## Roleplay
A manager says: "Several people find your tone dismissive in technical discussions." Ask for examples, paraphrase the concern, identify one behavior to test, and agree how progress can be observed.

## Homework
In three conversations, let the other person finish, ask two clarifying questions, and paraphrase before offering a solution. Give two pieces of appropriate SBI feedback and reflect.
