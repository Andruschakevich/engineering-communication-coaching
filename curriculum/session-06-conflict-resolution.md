# Session 6 — Conflict Resolution and Influencing

**Objectives:** Separate person from problem, identify shared goals, use evidence and trade-offs, and move disagreement toward a decision.

## Disagreement sequence
1. State the shared goal.
2. Summarize the other proposal fairly.
3. Ask which constraints or evidence matter.
4. Present your concern and supporting evidence.
5. Compare options and trade-offs.
6. Agree on a decision, experiment, owner, or escalation path.

## Roleplay
Two engineers strongly disagree about a technical approach. Clarify the opposing view, state your concern, explore alternatives, and close with a decision process. Increase difficulty with time pressure, interruptions, or new evidence.

## Homework
Use the sequence in one real technical discussion. Reflect: Did I understand the other position? Separate evidence from assumptions? Agree on a next step? What would I change?
