# Session 5 — Public Speaking and Presentations

**Objectives:** Structure a short presentation, maintain attention, speak clearly, and handle questions constructively.

## Presentation structure
1. Audience and purpose.
2. Problem or opportunity.
3. Why it matters.
4. Proposed approach.
5. Evidence, risks, and trade-offs.
6. Expected outcome.
7. Decision or next step.

## Practice
Deliver a five-minute technical presentation, e.g. "How we can improve our test automation architecture." Cover problem, importance, proposal, expected result, risks, and next steps. The coach asks challenging questions and occasionally interrupts.

## Feedback checklist
Was the objective clear? Was the structure easy to follow? Was detail appropriate? Were questions handled without defensiveness? Was there a clear conclusion?

## Optional Toastmasters activity
Visit two local club meetings as a guest; consider joining a suitable club. Practice prepared speeches and Table Topics (impromptu 1–2 minute responses). This complements, but does not replace, workplace practice.

## Homework
Record a five-minute presentation and self-review. Request one specific piece of feedback from a trusted colleague if possible.
