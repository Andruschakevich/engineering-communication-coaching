# Engineering Communication Coaching
## Kick-off & Coaching Agreement

**A practical eight-session development journey**

> The goal is not to change your personality. It is to make your communication as effective as your technical contribution.

# 2. Purpose and expectations

**This is:**
- A practical, supportive development process.
- Focused on observable communication behaviors.
- Built around reflection, practice, feedback, and small experiments.
- Adapted to the participant's goals and feedback.

**This is not:**
- A personality makeover or performance hearing.
- A requirement to agree with everyone.
- A promise of instant transformation.

- [ ] Purpose understood.
- [ ] the participant can challenge the format and suggest changes.
- [ ] Development goals are participant-owned.

---

# 3. The eight-session journey

| # | Session | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Self-awareness & communication assessment | Understand strengths, intent, impact, and priorities |
| 2 | Respectful communication & emotional intelligence | Stay direct and respectful under pressure |
| 3 | Active listening & constructive feedback | Listen, clarify, and give/receive feedback |
| 4 | Structured communication | Make messages clear, concise, and audience-aware |
| 5 | Public speaking & presentations | Present confidently and handle questions |
| 6 | Conflict resolution & influencing | Navigate disagreement and reach decisions |
| 7 | Leadership communication & professional presence | Facilitate and influence constructively |
| 8 | Review & 90-day development plan | Review evidence and sustain progress |

Typical format: 60 minutes weekly, with a small practical exercise between sessions.

- [ ] Session duration and cadence agreed.
- [ ] Preparation/practice time understood.
- [ ] Program can adapt based on progress.

# 5. Working agreement & confidentiality

**Working norms**
- Be candid without humiliation.
- Challenge behaviors and ideas, not personal worth.
- Ask questions before assuming intent.
- Respectful disagreement is welcome.
- Either person can pause or clarify.

**Information boundaries**
- Private reflections, detailed session notes, recordings, and transcripts are private by default.
- Manager updates are limited to what we explicitly agree.
- No raw work-message exports, screenshots, or verbatim private examples are shared.
- Legal and organizational obligations may limit confidentiality; explain these transparently.

Agree what may be shared with the manager:
- [ ] Attendance / participation
- [ ] Agreed development goals
- [ ] High-level progress
- [ ] Support or practice opportunities needed
- [ ] Other: ______________________
- [ ] No additional details without the participant's agreement, subject to policy and law.

# 6. How progress will be assessed

We will use three perspectives—not one overall score.

| Perspective | What it means |
|---|---|
| Self-assessment | the participant's own reflection and rating |
| Manager assessment | Manager's observations from normal work interactions |
| AI-assisted assessment | AI-supported review of permitted work communication, interpreted by the participant |

AI-assisted assessment is **not an objective verdict**. AI may miss context, misread tone, or overgeneralize.

- [ ] Explain the 1–5 / N.O. scale.
- [ ] Explain confidence: Low / Medium / High.
- [ ] Agree scores will not be averaged into a single rating.
- [ ] Differences between perspectives are discussion prompts.
- [ ] Evidence and behavior matter more than numbers.

---

# 7. AI-assisted review of work communication

**Proposed scope:** work communications from the previous six months, only if company policy permits this specific data and AI tool.

Before using Claude Code or another AI tool:
- [ ] Confirm company policy permits processing this data with this provider/tool.
- [ ] Confirm approved account, model, settings, retention, and data-handling requirements.
- [ ] Remember: Claude Code is not automatically local-only; selected context may be sent to the provider.
- [ ] Use only communications the participant is authorized to access and process.
- [ ] Exclude personal, customer-sensitive, HR, medical, legal, credential, and other restricted information.
- [ ] Do not bypass access controls or include conversations outside the authorized scope.

**Shared output only:** participant-reviewed ratings, confidence, sanitized patterns, paraphrased examples, limitations, and his interpretation.

**Never shared:** raw exports, screenshots, verbatim messages, names, project identifiers, or prompts containing raw messages.

- [ ] AI review permitted and participant agrees.
- [ ] Tool and scope agreed.
- [ ] the participant will review and approve the summary before sharing.

# 8. Self-reflection and personal goals

The participant also completes his own reflection, independently of the AI review.

2. Which are hardest?
3. What feedback have I heard more than once?
4. What happens when I feel rushed, challenged, or misunderstood?
5. What behavior would I like to change or strengthen?

Define 3–5 goals:
- What do I want to improve?
- Why does it matter?
- What behavior will I practice?
- In which situations?
- How will I measure progress?
- What support do I need?
