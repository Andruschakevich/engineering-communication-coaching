# Coaching Agreement

This template should be reviewed and adapted to the organization's policies and applicable requirements before use.

## Participants and purpose

**Participant:** [Name]  
**Coach:** [Name]  
**Sponsor / manager:** [Name or role]  
**Program:** Engineering Communication Coaching Program  
**Expected duration:** Eight sessions, normally weekly, 60 minutes each.

The purpose is to develop practical workplace communication behaviors through reflection, structured exercises, roleplay, and real-world experiments.

## Shared responsibilities

### Participant
- Take an active role in defining development goals.
- Attend sessions or provide reasonable notice when rescheduling.
- Try agreed experiments between sessions.
- Reflect honestly on outcomes and ask for clarification when feedback is unclear.
- Avoid sharing confidential third-party information unnecessarily.

### Coach
- Provide a respectful, structured, and challenging learning environment.
- Give feedback based on observable behavior and explain its impact.
- Protect coaching notes, recordings, and examples in accordance with agreed and organizational rules.
- Adapt exercises to the participant's needs and role.
- Be transparent about the limits of the coaching relationship.

### Manager / sponsor
- Continue normal management, feedback, and accountability responsibilities.
- Provide reasonable opportunities to practice new behaviors.
- Avoid requesting private session notes outside the agreed boundaries.
- Give feedback through normal, appropriate workplace channels.

## Recording and transcription

Recording is optional and requires explicit consent from every participant before each meeting.

Before recording, agree and document:
- What is recorded (audio/video) and whether a transcript is created.
- The tool/provider, including any third-party or AI processing.
- Whether the tool is approved for this purpose under company policy.
- Storage location and who has access.
- Retention period and deletion process.
- Whether recordings/transcripts may be used for preparation and coaching review.

Consent may be declined or withdrawn. If anyone declines, the meeting continues without recording and without penalty. Consent to recording does not automatically include consent to third-party AI transcription or analysis. Follow applicable law and organizational policy.

## Confidentiality and information sharing

**Private by default:** Personal reflections, detailed practice notes, recordings, transcripts, and specific examples discussed in sessions are not shared with the manager unless the participant agrees, subject to applicable law and organizational policy.

**Potential high-level updates (select and agree):**
- [ ] Attendance and participation.
- [ ] Agreed development goals.
- [ ] General progress against those goals.
- [ ] Support or opportunities needed from the manager.
- [ ] Other: [Specify].

**Not routinely shared:**
- Verbatim session notes or transcripts.
- Private reflections.
- Unnecessary details about third parties.
- Raw chat exports, screenshots, or message archives.
- AI prompts or raw AI outputs containing work messages.

**Limits:** Confidentiality is not absolute. The coach will follow applicable law and organizational policy for serious safety, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or other reportable concerns. Explain the relevant process before coaching begins.

## Use of work examples and AI-assisted review

- Use only data the participant is authorized to access and that company policy permits for this purpose.
- Confirm the AI provider, account, model, settings, retention, and data-handling requirements before processing work communications.
- Do not assume Claude Code or another AI coding assistant is local-only; selected context may be sent to the provider.
- Exclude personal conversations, customer secrets, credentials, HR/medical/legal matters, sensitive personal data, and any material the participant is not authorized to process.
- Do not bypass access controls or collect messages from spaces the participant is not authorized to access.
- Do not share raw exports, screenshots, verbatim messages, names, or project identifiers with the coach or manager.
- Share only a participant-reviewed summary, provisional ratings, confidence, and sanitized behavioral patterns.
- AI output is a hypothesis-generating input, not an objective verdict. The participant may challenge or contextualize it.
- Keep participant-specific notes separate from public or reusable program materials.

## Working norms

- Be candid without humiliation.
- Challenge ideas and behaviors, not personal worth.
- Ask questions before assuming intent.
- Either person may request a pause or clarification.
- Disagreement is allowed; respectful conduct is expected.
- The participant may raise concerns about the process.
