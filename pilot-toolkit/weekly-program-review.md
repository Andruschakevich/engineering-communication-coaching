# Weekly Program Review Template

Use this template after each coaching session to turn pilot experience into improvements to the reusable program. Keep participant identity and private details out of this document and out of the repository.

## 1. What happened?

Summarize the session at the level of the learning process:
- Which exercise or framework was used?
- Which parts engaged the participant?
- Where did the exercise stall or create confusion?
- Was the session completed within the planned time?

Do not include names, verbatim private messages, or identifiable incident narratives.

## 2. Learning evidence

Describe observable, anonymized evidence:
- What could the participant explain?
- What could the participant demonstrate?
- What changed after feedback and a second attempt?
- What remains difficult?
- Was there a real-world opportunity to apply the skill?

Separate direct observation from participant self-report.
