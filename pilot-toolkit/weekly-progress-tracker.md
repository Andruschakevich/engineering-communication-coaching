## 6. Coach reflection

- Did I create enough space for the participant's perspective?
- Was my feedback specific and fair?
- Did the practice reflect a real work challenge?
- Did I over-explain or solve the problem for the participant?
- What should I change next session?

## Weekly status

Choose one:
- **On track:** the participant is practicing and learning.
- **Needs adjustment:** the goal, exercise, or support needs to change.
- **Insufficient evidence:** not enough real opportunities or observations yet.

Do not interpret missed homework as proof of unwillingness. Explore context and agree on a realistic next step.
